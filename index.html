          About
          
Quality treated as an engineering discipline

        

      


      

        

          

            I am a results-driven Software Test Engineer with over 5 years of
            experience testing web-based applications across B2B e-commerce and
            e-auctions, enterprise procurement, national loyalty platforms, and global telecom
            messaging.
          

          

            My work covers the full testing lifecycle — requirement analysis, test planning and
            case design, functional, regression, smoke, sanity, system and integration execution,
            API validation in Postman, database verification in MySQL, and defect tracking through
            to closure in JIRA and TestRail. Alongside manual coverage I design and maintain
            Selenium WebDriver suites in Java using the Page Object Model, run
            through TestNG and Maven and triggered from Jenkins.
          

          

            My focus is the continuous improvement of testing processes —
            sharpening strategy, widening automation coverage, and folding performance and security
            checks into the sprint instead of bolting them on at the end.
